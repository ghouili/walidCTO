A fractional CTO in France or Western Europe typically costs between €3,000 and €10,000 a month for 1 to 2 days a week of senior technical leadership. That is roughly a third to a half of what a salaried CTO really costs a French startup once social charges, equity and recruitment are counted, and it is a different product from an agency or a freelance developer, so the comparison only works if you compare what each one actually delivers.

I am a fractional CTO myself, based in Tunis, working with founders in France and the rest of Europe. So read this with that bias in mind. I have tried to give honest market ranges, including the cases where another model is the better spend.

## What does a fractional CTO cost in 2026?

The short answer is a monthly retainer, priced on how many days a week you need. The ranges below are typical market ranges I see in France and neighbouring markets in 2026. They are not a survey, and your quotes will vary with seniority, sector and city.

- **Day rates** behind fractional CTO retainers usually sit between €700 and €1,500.
- **1 day a week** (about 4 days a month) lands roughly between €3,000 and €6,000 a month.
- **2 days a week** (about 8 to 9 days a month) lands roughly between €6,000 and €10,000 a month, sometimes more for very senior profiles in Paris.
- **Commitment** is usually a 3-month minimum, then month to month.

Nearshore fractional CTOs (Tunisia, Portugal, Eastern Europe) tend to sit in the lower half of those ranges while working in the same hours as Paris. Tunis is UTC+1 all year, so it shares the Paris clock in winter and is one hour behind in summer.

## The real cost of a salaried CTO in France

### Gross salary is only the start

For an early-stage startup in Paris, a CTO salary typically lands somewhere between €80,000 and €130,000 gross a year. Outside Paris it is often lower. Lyon, Nantes or Bordeaux usually come in 10 to 20% under Paris for the same profile, in my experience.

### Employer social charges

In France, the employer pays social charges on top of the gross salary. For a cadre, count roughly 40 to 45% on top. A €100,000 gross salary costs the company about €140,000 to €145,000 a year, or around €12,000 a month. Across the range above, the loaded cost sits between about €9,500 and €16,000 a month.

If your company qualifies as a Jeune Entreprise Innovante, part of those charges on R&D staff can be exempted. Check with your accountant, because the rules and caps change.

### Equity

A first CTO who joins after the founders usually negotiates equity, often as BSPCE. In my experience, that sits somewhere between 1% and 5% for a hire before product-market fit. A co-founder CTO is a different conversation, usually in double digits. Equity does not hit your bank account, but it is the most expensive thing you will ever give away if the hire does not work out.

### Recruitment and time to hire

A recruiter typically charges 15 to 25% of the first-year gross salary. Hiring a good CTO also takes time: three to six months is common, and a cadre in France usually has a three-month notice period on top. That is six months or more where nobody senior is making technical calls.

## What a fractional CTO retainer actually covers

A fractional CTO owns the decisions a full-time CTO would own, on the days you need them: architecture, code review, technical hiring, the roadmap and the conversation with investors. On my [fractional CTO engagements](/services/fractional-cto), that is 1 to 2 days a week with a 3-month minimum, and you own every decision, document and commit as it lands.

What you pay for is judgment, not hours of typing. On [Sabboura](/work/sabboura), a voice-first AI tutor for Tunisian bac students, the decision that mattered most was the cost model: lessons are generated once, reviewed by teachers, then replayed to every student, so live AI is only spent when a student asks a question. That one architecture call is what keeps AI costs predictable at student prices. No amount of extra developer hours would have found it.

The limit is real: 1 to 2 days a week is not a full-time engineer. If you need 40 hours of code a week, a fractional CTO alone is the wrong purchase. Most teams pair one with a developer or a small team.

## Agencies: priced per project, not per month

An agency sells a delivered build. In France, a custom MVP from an agency typically costs somewhere between €30,000 and €120,000, depending on scope. While the project runs, that often means €15,000 to €40,000 a month. Blended day rates usually sit between €500 and €900 per person.

You get a team, a project manager and a deadline. What you usually do not get is someone who stays after delivery to own the technical direction. I compare agencies, freelance CTOs and fractional CTOs in more detail in [this guide for pre-seed founders](/blog/fractional-cto-vs-freelance-vs-agency).

## Freelance senior developers: cheap per day, not per decision

On Malt and similar platforms, senior full-stack developers in France typically charge between €450 and €750 a day, more in Paris for niche stacks. Full time, that is roughly €9,000 to €15,000 a month.

A good senior freelancer writes excellent code. But a freelancer is usually hired to execute a backlog, not to decide what the backlog should be. If nobody owns architecture, you end up with fast output in a direction no one chose.

## The hidden costs nobody puts in the quote

The monthly figure is the visible part. These are the costs I see founders pay later.

- **The rewrite.** An MVP built without senior oversight often has to be rebuilt within 12 to 18 months. That is a second build, paid at full price, while competitors ship.
- **Lock-in.** If the agency holds the repository, the cloud account or the app store listing, leaving costs you weeks. Insist that you own code, infrastructure and accounts from day one.
- **Handover gaps.** When a freelancer or agency leaves without documentation, the next team spends its first month reading code instead of shipping.
- **Founder time.** A non-technical founder managing developers directly spends hours a week on decisions they cannot evaluate. That time has a price too.
- **Due diligence surprises.** Investors at seed check architecture, security, GDPR and how the team ships. Finding problems during the round is the most expensive time to find them. A [technical audit](/services/technical-audit) before you raise is cheaper than a delayed close.

## How to budget at each stage

- **Pre-seed, no product yet:** a fixed-scope MVP build plus light senior oversight. A fractional CTO at 1 day a week, or a fixed-price build with a clear handover.
- **Pre-seed with a first product and one or two developers:** a fractional CTO at 1 to 2 days a week is usually the best value per euro.
- **Seed, team of four or more:** fractional CTO while you recruit, then a full-time hire. A good fractional CTO helps you hire their own replacement.
- **Series A:** a full-time CTO, almost always.
